Title: Germline variant calling in a legacy exome cohort
Authors: E. Vasquez, M. Chen
Year: 2023
DOI: 10.1234/germline.2023.01

Abstract
We report germline short-variant calls across the 2019 exome cohort and show that
call counts are stable across coverage strata under a conservative base-quality
threshold.

Methods
Reads were aligned to the GRCh37 reference (human_g1k_v37). Short variants were
called with GATK HaplotypeCaller using a minimum base quality score of 20 and a
standard calling confidence threshold of 30. Final per-sample calls are written to
sample01.vcf and downstream joint-genotyping inputs.

Results
We identified 12,048 high-confidence germline variants in sample01 (Figure 2).
Variant counts were stable across coverage strata, supporting the choice of a base
quality threshold of 20 rather than 30.

Discussion
Retaining the GRCh37 build preserved comparability with previously published 2019
calls; a switch to GRCh38 would have required re-calling the full cohort.
