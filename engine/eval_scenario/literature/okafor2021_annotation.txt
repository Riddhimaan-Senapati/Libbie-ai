Title: Functional annotation of germline variants in cohortA
Authors: D. Okafor, P. Nair
Year: 2021
DOI: 10.1234/annot.2021.03

Abstract
We annotated the cohortA germline calls with Ensembl VEP cache version 105.

Methods
The germline VCF was annotated with Ensembl VEP using cache version 105, pinned to match the
GRCh38 assembly the calls were made on.

Results
Functional consequences were assigned to 3,142,880 variants in cohortA_annotated (Figure 1).
We found 214 predicted loss-of-function variants in known disease genes.

Discussion
Pinning the VEP cache version was necessary to keep annotations reproducible across re-runs.
